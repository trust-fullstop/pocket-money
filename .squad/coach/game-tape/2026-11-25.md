# Game tape — 2026-11-25

## Loans
The owner asked how to record a loan when a kid borrows money: repayment should come off next month's pay-out, and the kid should choose the repayment plan ("adult behaviour"). Shipped a 🤝 Loans feature:
- the kid asks, with their own plan and a live impact line;
- the parent approves, declines, offers a fee, or records a loan directly;
- repayments come from actual pocket-money rows;
- "Pay back now", forgive, and a dedicated undo;
- a pay-out loan line;
- privacy from grandparents.

- **Kryptonite before the build:** repayments must follow real allowance rows, not a copied schedule; never overdraw; one open loan per kid; mask loans for grandparents; lock order.
- **Vera's catches:**
  - a real deadlock: tick's family FOR UPDATE blocked FK key-share checks while kid RPCs held member locks. Fixed with FOR NO KEY UPDATE.
  - a privacy leak: the masked rows still said kind: loan_repayment.
  - an undo clash with a newer loan.
  Lesson: Postgres FK checks take KEY SHARE locks on the parent row, so never FOR UPDATE a parent row that other transactions insert children of while holding other locks.
