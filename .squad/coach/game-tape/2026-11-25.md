# Game tape — 2026-11-25

## Loans
The owner asked how to record a loan when a kid borrows money: repayment should come off next month's pay-out, and the kid should choose the repayment plan ("adult behaviour"). Shipped a 🤝 Loans feature:
- the kid asks, with their own plan and a live impact line;
- the parent approves, declines, offers a fee, or records a loan directly;
- repayments come from actual pocket-money rows;
- "Pay back now", forgive, and a dedicated undo;
- a pay-out loan line;
- privacy from grandparents.

- **Kryptonite before the build:** repayments must follow real allowance rows, not a copied schedule; never overdraw; one open loan per kid; mask loans for grandparents; lock order.
- **Vera's catches:**
  - a real deadlock: tick's family FOR UPDATE blocked FK key-share checks while kid RPCs held member locks. Fixed with FOR NO KEY UPDATE.
  - a privacy leak: the masked rows still said kind: loan_repayment.
  - an undo clash with a newer loan.
  Lesson: Postgres FK checks take KEY SHARE locks on the parent row, so never FOR UPDATE a parent row that other transactions insert children of while holding other locks.

## Night: loans visible everywhere + December series (1ac0b32)
The owner recorded a loan and couldn't see it in the Pay-out or in "How X is doing": with no repayment yet, the loan only lived in a grey footnote, and repayments were hidden inside Spent. Fix: a real loan panel per child in the Pay-out (lent, paid back, still to go in euros, planned repayment, next pocket money preview, family total owed, Record a loan shortcut) and Borrowed / Paid back lines in the tallies. Parallel build: the December Money school series "Borrowing and insurance" (6 tracked lessons, loan calculator, opens 1 Dec in the family time zone, parents preview).
Kryptonite caught 6 design gaps before the build: undo needs a signed number, family-timezone bounds, a new lesson kind would vanish in core-only code paths, listed vs openable for the date gate (relatives too), and a capped 'planned' repayment. Vera passed first time. Her non-blocking notes were fixed before shipping; the most useful was that lesson amounts sized for rand read as absurd in euros, so amounts now scale by currency.
Lesson: when a feature's main effect is in the future (a repayment on the next pocket money day), show the plan and its effect, not just the history.
